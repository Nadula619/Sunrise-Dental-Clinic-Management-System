Clinic Staff Operating Manual & Help

Standard operating procedures (SOP) and step-by-step instructions for clinic receptionists

Welcome to the Sunrise Dental Clinic System

This computerized patient management system replaces manual notebook records to eliminate double-bookings, billing errors, and lost files. Follow the standard procedures below to register patients, manage schedules, and process payments.

1 User Authentication & Role-Based Access

Logging into the System:

  • Navigate to the login portal (login.html).
  • Enter your assigned authorized staff username and password.
  • Authorized roles include Administrator, Receptionist, and Dentist.
  • Passwords are securely encrypted with BCrypt salted hashing.

Safe System Exit: Click the red logout icon in the bottom-left profile panel to invalidate your secure session before leaving the terminal.

2 Registering a New Patient Appointment

To register a walk-in or phone-in booking:

  1. Click the "Register New Appointment" button in the top navigation or on the Appointments page.
  2. Enter the Patient Full Name (letters only, min 2 characters).
  3. Enter a valid 10-digit Sri Lankan phone number (e.g. 0771234567).
  4. Select the attending Dentist from the roster.
  5. Select the required Treatment Procedure (base cost will be retrieved automatically).
  6. Choose the Appointment Date and Time Slot (operating hours 08:00 to 20:00).
  7. Click Confirm & Book Appointment. The system will automatically check for doctor conflicts and generate a unique tracking ID (e.g., APT-2026-0005).
Conflict Prevention: If a doctor already has a booked visit at the same date and time, the system will block the submission and alert you to select another slot.
3 Searching and Looking Up Patient Records

To find an existing booking or patient history:

  • Use the live search bar on the Appointments page. You can search by Appointment Number (e.g. APT-2026-0001), Patient Name, Contact Number, or Dentist.
  • Filter by Attending Dentist, Booking Status (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled), or specific Date.
  • Click the icon to open the complete patient card showing contact address, clinical notes, and doctor allocation.
4 Calculating Treatment Fees & Printing Receipts

To issue an official patient receipt:

  1. Navigate to the Billing & Invoices page and click "Create New Invoice" (or click the receipt icon next to any appointment).
  2. Select the target appointment. Patient, doctor, procedure, and base treatment fees load automatically.
  3. Add any Extra Charges (e.g. specialized medication, x-rays, disposables).
  4. Select the patient's Discount Strategy:
    • Standard: 0% discount.
    • Senior Citizen: 10% discount on total subtotal.
    • Insurance Partner: 15% discount coverage.
    • Promotional / Loyalty: 5% discount.
  5. Choose payment method (Cash, Card, or Online Transfer) and save.
  6. Click "Print Receipt" to preview and output a clean, formatted receipt suitable for A4 or thermal POS printers.
5 Viewing Operational Reports & Decision Support

The Management Reports module provides high-level clinic intelligence:

  • Realized Gross Revenue: Aggregate cash flow from settled visits.
  • Dentist Workload & Performance: Comparative breakdown of patients treated and revenue generated per doctor.
  • Procedure Popularity: Ranking of dental treatments (Root Canals, Scaling, Whitening, Extractions).
  • Daily Schedule Sheets: Printable daily agenda for each surgery room.
